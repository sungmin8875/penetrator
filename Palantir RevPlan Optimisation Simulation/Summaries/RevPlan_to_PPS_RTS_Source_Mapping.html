Execution playbook

  1. Build the model-key crosswalk first — highest risk.The engine keys on model_id = PPS MODEL_NO (MGC5C013). RTS & ERP tables key on PRODID / MODEL_CODE (SPSNP4001K.K040). Establish and validate MODEL_NO ↔ PRODID — most likely via PK1_MODEL.SAP_CODE / CUSTOMER_MODEL_NO — before anything else joins.
  2. Swap the 9 direct inputs to PPS/RTS.Biggest wins: RTSP_WIP_N replaces both wip_lots and process_steps; PK1_MODEL replaces model_master + model_unit_conversion; PK1_MPLAN replaces revenue_plan; PK1_BOM, PK1_MATERIAL, PK1_EQUIPMENT cover the rest.
  3. For the 5 partials, take the structure from PPS/RTS and keep deriving the analytics columns.e.g. routing structure from RTSP_MODEL_ROUTING_M + lead times from PK1_MODEL_ROUTE, but continue computing the per-panel/second event-time fields. Reconstruct the Korean equipment-group taxonomy for capacity mapping.
  4. Convert currency through PK1_EX_RATE.The engine expects sales_unit_price_krw; PK1_MODEL.SALES_PRICE / ODS_ERP_MODEL_PRICE.LIST_PRICE are frequently USD/CNY.
  5. Leave the 3 gaps on their current feeds.equipment_constraints (business-rule overlay), planned_material_arrivals (needs a procurement/PO ETA feed), and sales_order_lines (needs an ERP sales-order feed) have no equivalent in the core set.
  6. Re-profile on full extracts before cut-over.The EDA stats come from ~100-row samples; null% / uniqueness are indicative only. Validate sparse columns (vendor lead time, price currency) at full volume.

5Three caveats that will make or break the swap

1
Model-key crosswalk is mandatory.

Engine model_id = PPS MODEL_NO (MGC5C013, ZGS76011). RTS RTSP_WIP_N/RTSP_MODEL_ROUTING_M use PRODID, and ERP inventory uses MODEL_CODE/ITEM_CODE — both SPSNP4001K.K040 form. Map via PK1_MODEL.SAP_CODE / CUSTOMER_MODEL_NO (the current model.txt already carries sap_code/customer_model_no). Validate this first — highest-risk item.

2
Currency.

Engine wants sales_unit_price_krw; PK1_MODEL.SALES_PRICE and ODS_ERP_MODEL_PRICE.LIST_PRICE are often USD/CNY → convert through PK1_EX_RATE.

3
EDA stats are sample-only.

Profiling is from ~100-row samples, not full tables. Null% / uniqueness are indicative — re-profile on full extracts before committing, especially sparse columns (VENDOR_LEAD_TIME, price currencies).
